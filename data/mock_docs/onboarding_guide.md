# New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Contoso Corporation | People & Culture**  
**Welcome to the Team!**

---

## Welcome Message

Congratulations on joining Contoso Corporation! We are delighted to have you on board. This guide will walk you through your first 90 days and help you get settled in as quickly and comfortably as possible.

Your success matters to us. This guide covers what to expect on Day 1, your first week, first month, and first 90 days, along with key contacts and resources.

---

## Day 1 Checklist

### Before You Arrive (sent to your personal email)
- [ ] Sign and return your contract and NDA
- [ ] Complete right-to-work documentation
- [ ] Provide bank details for payroll
- [ ] Read the Welcome Pack (attached)

### When You Arrive
Your manager or a buddy will meet you at reception.

1. **Building Access**: Collect your ID badge from reception (bring photo ID).
2. **IT Setup**: Visit the IT desk to receive your laptop, access credentials, and phone (if applicable).
3. **Account Setup**:
   - Log in to Microsoft 365 at portal.office.com with your company email (`firstname.lastname@contoso.com`)
   - Set up MFA on your phone (Microsoft Authenticator)
   - Change your temporary password immediately
4. **Email Signature**: Download the Contoso email signature template from the IT SharePoint library and configure it in Outlook.
5. **Team Introduction**: Your manager will introduce you to the team.

## First Week

### Systems Access
Work with IT (helpdesk@contoso.com or ext. 4100) to request access to:
- SharePoint sites relevant to your role
- Any line-of-business applications (confirm with your manager)
- VPN (required for remote access)

### Mandatory Training (complete within first week)
All new employees must complete the following e-learning modules in the Learning Portal:
1. **Information Security Awareness** (~45 min)
2. **Data Protection & GDPR** (~30 min)
3. **Code of Conduct** (~20 min)
4. **Health & Safety** (~20 min)

Access the Learning Portal via: `https://learn.contoso.com`

### Key SharePoint Sites to Bookmark
| Site | URL | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| HR Hub | `/sites/hr` | Policies, leave requests, benefits |
| IT Support | `/sites/itsupport` | IT guides, software requests |
| Company News | `/sites/news` | Announcements, newsletters |
| Your Department Site | Ask your manager | Team documents |

## First Month

### 1:1 with Your Manager
Schedule weekly 1:1s with your manager to:
- Align on priorities and expectations
- Discuss your 30/60/90 day goals
- Address any questions or concerns

### Meet Key Stakeholders
Work with your manager to schedule introductory calls with:
- Your team members
- Key internal customers or partner teams
- The HR Business Partner for your department

### Review Your Role Profile
Your manager should share your formal job description and KPIs. If you have not received these by the end of week 2, contact HR.

---

## First 90 Days Goals

By the end of your first 90 days you should:
1. **Understand** the company strategy, your team's goals, and how your role contributes.
2. **Complete** all mandatory training modules.
3. **Establish** regular communication rhythms with your manager and team.
4. **Deliver** at least one meaningful piece of work (agree specifics with your manager).
5. **Provide** feedback on your onboarding experience via the Onboarding Survey (link sent at day 85).

# Q1 2024 Business Performance Report

**Contoso Corporation | Finance & Strategy**  
**Confidential – Internal Use Only**  
**Period: January 1 – March 31, 2024**

---

## Executive Summary

Contoso Corporation delivered a strong first quarter in 2024, with total revenue of **£42.3 million**, representing 8.2% growth year-over-year. EBITDA margin improved to **22.4%** (Q1 2023: 19.8%), driven by operational efficiencies and favourable product mix shift. We ended the quarter with **1,247 active customers**, a net increase of 67 from Q4 2023.

Key highlights:
- Cloud Services division grew 31% YoY – now representing 48% of total revenue.
- Professional Services revenue was broadly flat (+2%) as planned during our service transition.
- Recurring revenue (ARR) reached **£38.1 million**, up from £33.4 million in Q1 2023.
- Employee headcount reached 892 full-time equivalents (FTE), with 34 net new hires in the quarter.

## 4. Operational Highlights

### 4.1 Product
- **ContosoCRM v8.2** launched February 2024 — first release with AI-assisted workflow automation. Adoption: 34% of enterprise customers to date.
- **Data Vault (cloud storage module)** entered general availability March 2024. 156 customer activations in first month.

### 4.2 Sales & Marketing
- **Pipeline**: Total qualified pipeline stands at £87.4M (2.1x Q1 revenue).
- **Win rate** improved to 31% from 26% in Q1 2023, attributed to improved competitive positioning and updated ROI calculator tool.
- **Marketing-sourced leads** increased 22% QoQ following launch of the new content marketing programme.

### 4.3 People
- Total headcount: 892 FTE (Q4 2023: 858 FTE)
- New hires: 34 (Engineering: 18, Sales: 9, Support: 7)
- Voluntary attrition: 2.8% annualised (industry benchmark: 12%)
- Employee engagement score (last survey, Feb 2024): 78% favourable

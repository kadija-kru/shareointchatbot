# Benefits & Compensation Guide

**Contoso Corporation | Total Rewards**  
**Effective Date: January 2024**

---

## Overview

This guide explains the full range of benefits and compensation elements available to Contoso employees. All benefits described here apply to permanent employees working 20+ hours per week unless otherwise stated.

For part-time employees (fewer than 20 hours per week), please contact HR for a pro-rated benefits summary.

---

## 1. Base Salary

Salaries are reviewed annually in January. Salary decisions are based on:
- Individual performance rating (year-end review)
- Market benchmark data (Willis Towers Watson survey)
- Internal pay equity analysis
- Company financial performance

Salary changes take effect on **1 April** each year. Employees who join after 1 October are not eligible for the annual review cycle until the following year.

---

## 2. Annual Bonus

Eligible employees (Grade 4 and above) participate in the Annual Bonus Plan:

| Grade | Target Bonus (% of salary) |
|---|---|
| Grade 4 | 10% |
| Grade 5 | 15% |
| Grade 6 | 20% |
| Grade 7+ | 25–40% |

Bonus is split: 70% company performance / 30% individual performance.  
Bonuses are paid in March for the prior year. A minimum of 6 months' continuous employment and being in good standing at the time of payment are required.

---

## 3. Equity / Long-Term Incentive Plan (LTIP)

Senior employees (Grade 6+) are eligible for stock options or restricted stock units (RSUs).

- **Vesting**: 4 years, with a 1-year cliff (25% vests after year 1, then monthly).
- **Grant frequency**: Annual, in February.
- For details on exercising options, contact Finance at equity@contoso.com.

## 4. Health & Wellbeing

### 4.1 Private Health Insurance
Contoso provides private health cover for all employees via Bupa:
- Company pays **80%** of the premium.
- Employee pays remaining 20% via monthly payroll deduction.
- Coverage: inpatient, outpatient, specialist consultations, mental health, dental (optional add-on).
- Dependants can be added; employee pays 100% of the additional premium.

Enrolment deadline: **30 days from start date**.

### 4.2 Life Assurance
All employees receive life assurance of **4× annual salary**. Nominate your beneficiary in the HR portal.

### 4.3 Income Protection
Long-term income protection provides **66% of salary** after a 13-week absence, up to age 65.

### 4.4 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
Free, confidential support 24/7:
- Counselling (up to 8 sessions per year)
- Legal advice
- Financial coaching
- Manager support resources

**EAP Provider**: Supportline | Free phone: **0800-555-0200** | Website: eap.contoso.com

### 4.5 Gym & Wellness Allowance
Employees receive a **£50/month** wellness allowance (reimbursable) for gym memberships, fitness apps, or other qualifying wellbeing activities. Submit claims quarterly through the HR portal.

## 5. Pension / Retirement

### UK Employees (Workplace Pension)
- Contoso contributes **5% of qualifying earnings**.
- Employee minimum contribution: 3% (can increase voluntarily).
- Provider: Aviva (Contoso Master Trust).
- Enrolment is automatic from day 1; opt-out must be done within 3 months.

### US Employees (401k)
- Contoso matches **100% of contributions up to 5%** of salary.
- Immediate vesting of company match.
- Provider: Fidelity NetBenefits.
- Enrolment window: within 30 days of start date.

---

## 6. Learning & Development

- **Annual L&D Budget**: £1,500 (UK) / $2,000 (US) per employee per year.
- Covers: external training, conferences, certifications, books/subscriptions.
- Approval required from manager before booking (HR portal).
- Budget resets on **1 January** each year; unused budget does not carry over.

### Certifications
Role-critical certifications (e.g., AWS, Azure, PMP, CISSP) may be fully funded by Contoso outside the standard L&D budget with business justification. Discuss with your manager and HR.

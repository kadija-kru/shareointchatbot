# Human Resources Policy Handbook

**Contoso Corporation | HR Department**  
**Version 3.2 | Effective January 2024**

---

## 1. Employment Policies

### 1.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
Contoso Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, age, or any other characteristic protected by law. All employment decisions are based on qualifications, merit, and business need.

### 1.2 Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Flexible working arrangements may be requested through your line manager and require HR approval. Core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM) must be observed unless a formal flexible working agreement is in place.

### 1.3 Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week subject to manager approval and role suitability. Remote workers must ensure a safe, dedicated workspace, maintain data security standards, and be available during core hours.

---

## 2. Leave Entitlements

### 2.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues from the first day of employment at 2.08 days per month. Unused leave may be carried over (maximum 5 days) into the following year.

### 2.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required. Sick leave does not roll over into the next calendar year.

### 2.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave. Employees must notify HR at least 8 weeks before the anticipated start date.

### 2.4 Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all statutory public holidays in their country of employment. If required to work on a public holiday, employees receive double pay or a day off in lieu.

---

## 3. Performance Management

### 3.1 Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted twice yearly: mid-year (June) and end-of-year (December). Each employee completes a self-assessment, followed by a manager review and a calibration session.

### 3.2 Performance Improvement Plans
If performance concerns arise outside the review cycle, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be initiated. A PIP will include specific, measurable targets with a 90-day timeframe.

---

## 4. Code of Conduct

All employees must adhere to Contoso's Code of Conduct:
- Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect.
- Maintain confidentiality of company and client information.
- Report suspected misconduct to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any outside employment to HR.

---

## 5. Grievance Procedure

Employees who have a grievance should:
1. Raise the issue informally with their line manager.
2. If unresolved, submit a formal grievance in writing to HR.
3. An investigation will be conducted within 15 working days.
4. The outcome will be communicated in writing, with a right of appeal.
